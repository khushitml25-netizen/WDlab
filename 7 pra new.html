Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India

About

Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam was the 11th President of India and one of the most respected scientists in the country. He played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs. Known as the "Missile Man of India", he inspired millions of students through his speeches and books.

Major Achievements

  • 11th President of India (2002–2007)
  • Lead scientist in India's missile development
  • Recipient of Bharat Ratna
  • Author of famous books like "Wings of Fire"

Legacy

Dr. Kalam will always be remembered for his contribution to science, education, and inspiring the youth of India to dream big and work hard.
